UPYA Hosts Youth Entrepreneurship Session

23 May 2026 Malela Village, Seme

Uwezo Pamoja Youths Association (UPYA) brought young people together in Malela Village for a practical entrepreneurship and financial empowerment session designed to encourage young people to turn ideas into meaningful opportunities.

Full Story

The session, held on 23 May 2026, created an opportunity for young people to share ideas, learn from one another and explore practical approaches to entrepreneurship.

The programme focused on the belief that young people can become active contributors to the development of their communities when they are equipped with knowledge, confidence, mentorship and opportunities.

Participants engaged in conversations around identifying opportunities, developing ideas, understanding financial responsibility and building sustainable initiatives within their communities.

What Happened

Youth Engagement Young people came together to discuss opportunities, challenges and ideas that can improve their livelihoods.
Entrepreneurship Participants explored how simple ideas can be developed into practical community initiatives.
Financial Awareness The discussions encouraged responsible financial thinking and planning.
Networking The event created an opportunity for participants to meet, share experiences and build relationships.

Impact

Beyond the event itself, UPYA aims to ensure that youth empowerment becomes a continuous process. The organisation continues to create platforms for mentorship, learning, collaboration and community action.

What's Next?

UPYA will continue creating opportunities for young people to participate in mentorship, entrepreneurship, leadership, education, climate action and community development activities.

Young people who would like to participate in future activities are encouraged to follow UPYA's updates and get involved in upcoming programmes.
