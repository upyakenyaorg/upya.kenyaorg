UPYA Hosts Youth Entrepreneurship Session

23 May 2026 | Malela Village

Introduction

Uwezo Pamoja Youths Association brought young people together in Malela Village for a practical conversation on entrepreneurship, financial literacy and opportunities for young people.

The session created an opportunity for participants to share ideas, learn from one another and explore practical ways of turning ideas into sustainable opportunities.

Full Story

The event, themed From Ideas to Income, focused on helping young people understand how ideas can be developed into practical initiatives.

Participants engaged in conversations around entrepreneurship, financial management, identifying opportunities and working together to solve challenges within their communities.

The gathering also provided a platform for young people to connect, ask questions and learn from experiences shared by other participants and community members.

What Happened

  • Youth participated in entrepreneurship discussions.
  • Participants explored practical income-generating ideas.
  • The session encouraged financial awareness.
  • Young people shared experiences and ideas.
  • Participants discussed the importance of teamwork and community support.

Impact

The session contributed to creating a space where young people could think about their potential, exchange ideas and identify practical pathways towards economic participation.

Beyond the individual participants, conversations like these strengthen a culture of young people supporting one another and taking responsibility for community development.

What's Next

UPYA will continue creating opportunities for young people to access mentorship, skills, entrepreneurship conversations and community-based programmes.

The association welcomes young people, mentors, organisations and partners who want to contribute to practical youth development.
