The Story

Creating space for young people to grow.

UPYA believes that young people need more than information. They need people who listen, encourage, guide and help them see possibilities beyond their current circumstances.

Through school visits, mentorship forums and youth engagements, UPYA connects young people with mentors and community voices who can share experiences, practical knowledge and encouragement.

The programme encourages young people to think about education, leadership, personal development, careers, entrepreneurship and their role in transforming their communities.

Mentorship Areas

What young people explore.

Education & Careers

Encouraging learners to take education seriously while exploring career paths and future opportunities.

Personal Development

Building confidence, communication, discipline, decision-making and positive character.

Leadership

Helping young people understand their ability to influence positive change in their schools and communities.

Impact

A conversation can change a direction.

Through mentorship, young people receive an opportunity to interact with people who can help them think differently about their future.
