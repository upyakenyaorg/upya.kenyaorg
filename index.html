About UPYA

Uwezo Pamoja Youths Association (UPYA) is a youth-led community organization dedicated to empowering young people through mentorship, entrepreneurship, leadership development, civic engagement and sustainable community service.

Who We Are

UPYA believes that every young person has unlimited potential. We exist to empower, equip, mentor and nurture tomorrow's world by creating opportunities for growth, innovation and community transformation.

Mission

To empower, equip, mentor and nurture tomorrow's world.

Vision

A generation of empowered youth transforming communities into centers of lasting positive change.

Our Core Values

These principles guide every project, decision and partnership.

Teamwork

Working together to achieve lasting impact.

Integrity

Doing what is right with honesty and accountability.

Transparency

Building trust through openness and responsibility.

Equity

Equal opportunities for every young person.

Innovation

Encouraging creativity and practical solutions.

Community Impact

Creating sustainable change for generations.

Our Programs

UPYA empowers communities through impactful youth-centered programs.

Education & Mentorship

Career guidance, academic mentorship, leadership training and life skills.

Entrepreneurship

Helping young people create businesses, employment and financial independence.

Environmental Action

Tree planting, clean-up campaigns and climate awareness initiatives.

Sports Development

Sports tournaments promoting peace, unity and talent development.

Civic Education

Building responsible citizens through governance and leadership education.

Community Service

Giving back through volunteering, outreach and humanitarian initiatives.
