About UPYA

Uwezo Pamoja Youths Association brings young people together to learn, lead, innovate and create lasting positive change.

🎯

Our Mission

To empower, equip, mentor and nurture tomorrow’s world.

🌍

Our Vision

A generation of empowered youth transforming communities into centers of lasting positive change.

🤝

Our Values

Teamwork, integrity, transparency, accountability and equity.

Our Programs

Programs that develop skills, encourage participation and strengthen communities.

🎓

Education & Mentorship

Career guidance, student mentorship, leadership and academic support.

💡

Entrepreneurship

Financial literacy, business skills and pathways from ideas to income.

🗣️

Youth Dialogue

Debates, civic education and safe spaces for youth voices.

🌱

Environment

Tree planting and sustainable community action.

🛡️

Social Awareness

Education and awareness on issues affecting young people.

🎥

Media & Storytelling

Sharing youth stories, opportunities and community impact.
