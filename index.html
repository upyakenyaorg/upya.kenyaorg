WHO WE ARE

Building Tomorrow's Leaders Today.

Uwezo Pamoja Youths Association (UPYA) is a youth-led community organization committed to empowering young people and creating positive change within our communities.

UPYA Youth for Change
OUR PURPOSE

Creating opportunities. Inspiring leadership. Driving change.

We believe that young people are not simply leaders of tomorrow. They are leaders of today. UPYA creates platforms where young people can discover their potential, develop practical skills and contribute meaningfully to society.

Our Mission

To empower, equip, mentor and nurture tomorrow's world.

Our Vision

A generation of empowered youth transforming communities into centres of lasting positive change.
