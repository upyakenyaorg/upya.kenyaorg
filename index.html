ABOUT UPYA

Building Tomorrow's Leaders Today

Uwezo Pamoja Youths Association (UPYA) is a youth-led community organization dedicated to empowering, equipping, mentoring and nurturing young people through education, leadership, innovation, entrepreneurship, sports and community service. Our goal is to develop responsible leaders who create lasting positive change within their communities.

Mission

To empower, equip, mentor and nurture tomorrow's world.

Vision

A generation of empowered youth transforming communities into centres of lasting positive change.

Core Values

Teamwork Integrity Transparency Accountability Equity
OUR PROGRAMS

Empowering Youth Through Action

Our programs are designed to develop skills, inspire leadership, promote innovation, and create opportunities that transform lives and communities.

Education & Mentorship

Career guidance, academic support, leadership mentoring and life skills development.

Entrepreneurship

Business training, financial literacy and supporting youth-led enterprises.

Sports Development

Using sports to promote unity, health, discipline and talent development.

Environment

Tree planting, environmental conservation and climate awareness campaigns.

Civic Education

Empowering youth with knowledge on governance, democracy and active citizenship.

Digital Skills

Training in technology, digital literacy, online opportunities and innovation.
