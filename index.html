WHO WE ARE

Building Tomorrow's Leaders Today

Uwezo Pamoja Youths Association (UPYA) is a youth-led community organization dedicated to empowering young people and creating positive change within our communities.

Through mentorship, education, entrepreneurship, sports, civic engagement, innovation and community service, we create opportunities for young people to discover their potential and become responsible leaders.

Our Mission

To empower, equip, mentor and nurture tomorrow's world.

Our Vision

A generation of empowered youth transforming communities into centres of lasting positive change.

WHAT WE DO

Our Programs

We turn ideas into action through practical programs designed to empower young people and strengthen communities.

Education & Mentorship

Academic support, career guidance, leadership development, life skills and student mentorship.

Entrepreneurship

Business skills, financial literacy, innovation and support for youth-led income generating ideas.

Sports & Talent

Using sports and talent to promote unity, discipline, health, teamwork and youth development.

Environment

Tree planting, environmental conservation and youth-led climate action.

Civic Education

Equipping young people with knowledge about governance, leadership, rights and responsible citizenship.

Digital Skills

Digital literacy, technology skills, online opportunities and youth innovation.
