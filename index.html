WHO WE ARE

About UPYA

Uwezo Pamoja Youths Association (UPYA) is a youth-led organization committed to empowering, equipping, mentoring and nurturing young people to become responsible leaders who transform their communities through innovation, education and service.

Mission

To empower, equip, mentor and nurture tomorrow's world through sustainable programs that transform lives and communities.

Vision

A generation of empowered youth transforming communities into centres of lasting positive change.

Core Values

  • ✔ Teamwork
  • ✔ Integrity
  • ✔ Transparency
  • ✔ Accountability
  • ✔ Equity
