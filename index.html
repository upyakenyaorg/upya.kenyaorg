WHO WE ARE

Building Tomorrow's Leaders Today.

Uwezo Pamoja Youths Association (UPYA) is a youth-led community organization dedicated to empowering, equipping, mentoring and nurturing young people through education, leadership, innovation, entrepreneurship, sports and community service.

UPYA Youth-led. Community-focused.

Our Purpose

We believe that young people are not simply leaders of tomorrow — they are partners in creating change today. UPYA creates platforms where young people can discover their potential, develop skills and contribute meaningfully to their communities.

Our Mission

To empower, equip, mentor and nurture tomorrow's world.

Our Vision

A generation of empowered youth transforming communities into centres of lasting positive change.

Our Core Values

Teamwork Integrity Transparency Accountability Equity
