WHO WE ARE

Young people at the centre of community transformation.

Uwezo Pamoja Youths Association (UPYA) is a youth-focused community organization committed to empowering, equipping, nurturing and mentoring young people to become active contributors to the development of their communities.

We create opportunities for young people to develop their skills, discover their potential, participate in community development and take part in initiatives addressing social, educational, environmental and economic challenges.

Through partnerships, mentorship, community action, skills development and youth-led initiatives, UPYA seeks to create an environment where young people can turn ideas into meaningful action.

OUR STORY

From an idea to a growing movement.

UPYA's journey is built around a simple belief: when young people are empowered and given opportunities, they can become powerful agents of positive change.

Founded

UPYA was founded with a vision of bringing young people together to create opportunities, develop leadership and contribute to positive community development.

First Initiatives

In its early stages, UPYA focused on bringing young people together through mentorship, community engagement, sports, volunteering and activities that encouraged teamwork and personal development.

Community Outreach

As UPYA grew, its activities expanded into education, civic participation, environmental initiatives, health awareness, mentorship and other programmes responding to the needs of young people and their communities.

Partnerships

UPYA continues to build relationships with schools, universities, community organizations, institutions and other stakeholders. These partnerships help create opportunities for collaboration, learning and meaningful community impact.

Current Programmes

Today, UPYA works around youth empowerment, education, health, gender-based violence awareness, climate action, entrepreneurship, civic participation and community development.

Future Vision

UPYA is building towards a generation of empowered young people who take initiative, develop solutions and transform their communities into centres of lasting positive change.
