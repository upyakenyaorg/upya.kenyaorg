About Us

Empowering Young People. Transforming Communities.

Uwezo Pamoja Youths Association (UPYA) is a youth-focused community organization committed to empowering, equipping, nurturing and mentoring young people to become active contributors to the development of their communities.

Who We Are

Young people with the power to shape their communities.

Uwezo Pamoja Youths Association (UPYA) is a youth-focused community organization committed to empowering, equipping, nurturing and mentoring young people to become active contributors to the development of their communities.

We create opportunities for young people to develop their skills, discover their potential, participate in community development and take part in initiatives addressing social, educational, environmental and economic challenges.
