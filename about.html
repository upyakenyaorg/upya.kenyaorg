About UPYA

Empowering, Equipping, Mentoring and Nurturing Tomorrow's World

Who We Are

Uwezo Pamoja Youths Association (UPYA) is a youth-led community organization committed to empowering young people through education, leadership, mentorship, innovation, entrepreneurship, sports, environmental conservation and community service.

We believe every young person has potential. Through collaboration, training and mentorship we create opportunities that transform lives and strengthen communities.

Mission

To empower, equip, mentor and nurture tomorrow's world.

Vision

A generation of empowered youth transforming communities into centers of lasting positive change.

Core Values

Teamwork • Integrity • Transparency • Accountability • Equity
