Who can get involved?

UPYA is bigger than membership.

You do not have to be a registered member to contribute to the UPYA movement. We welcome people and institutions who share our commitment to youth empowerment and community transformation.

Young People

Participate, learn, lead and contribute to community development.

Professionals

Offer skills, mentorship and technical expertise.

Community Members

Support local initiatives and community activities.

Schools & Institutions

Collaborate on education, mentorship, climate and youth programmes.

Organizations & Businesses

Partner with UPYA on projects and community development.

Friends & Supporters
