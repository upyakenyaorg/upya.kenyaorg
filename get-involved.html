How to contribute:

  1. Open M-Pesa on your phone.
  2. Select Lipa na M-Pesa.
  3. Select Pay Bill.
  4. Enter the UPYA Paybill number.
  5. Enter UPYA as the account number.
  6. Enter the amount and confirm.
