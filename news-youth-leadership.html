Building Young Leaders

Youth Leadership Seme

UPYA believes that the future of communities depends on young people who are prepared to take responsibility, serve others and contribute positively to society. Through leadership activities, mentorship and community engagement, UPYA seeks to nurture young leaders with purpose.

Full Story

Leadership is more than holding a position. It involves responsibility, integrity, teamwork, accountability and the willingness to serve others.

UPYA creates opportunities for young people to develop these qualities through practical activities, discussions, mentorship and participation in community initiatives.

Young people are encouraged to identify challenges around them and think about constructive ways of responding to those challenges.

What Happened

Leadership Conversations Young people engaged in discussions about responsibility, integrity and the role of youth in their communities.
Teamwork Participants worked together and learned how collaboration can strengthen community initiatives.
Responsibility Young people were encouraged to take responsibility for their decisions and actions.
Community Participation Participants explored ways of using their skills and ideas to contribute positively to their communities.

Impact

Developing young leaders strengthens communities by creating people who are prepared to participate, collaborate and take responsibility. UPYA seeks to nurture leadership that is based on service, integrity and positive community impact.

What's Next?

UPYA will continue creating platforms where young people can develop leadership skills, interact with mentors and gain practical experience through community activities.

The organisation also encourages young people who have gained experience through UPYA activities to share their knowledge and encourage others.
