Climate Action

Youth and Climate Action

Environment Youth Participation Seme

Climate change affects communities in many ways, and young people have an important role to play in building a more sustainable future. UPYA encourages youth participation in environmental awareness, climate innovation and practical community action.

Full Story

Climate action is not only about global conversations. It also begins at community level, where young people can identify environmental challenges and explore practical solutions.

UPYA seeks to create spaces where young people can learn about environmental issues, exchange ideas and participate in activities that promote sustainable communities.

Through awareness, innovation and community participation, young people can contribute to solutions that protect the environment while creating opportunities for future generations.

What Happened

Climate Awareness Young people were encouraged to understand how environmental challenges affect their communities.
Youth Innovation Participants were encouraged to think about practical and innovative approaches to environmental challenges.
Community Participation The initiative promoted the involvement of young people in activities that contribute to healthier communities.
Sustainable Thinking Participants were encouraged to consider how today's actions can affect future generations.

Impact

Climate action creates opportunities for young people to become environmental stewards and innovators. By increasing awareness and encouraging practical action, UPYA seeks to contribute to communities that are more environmentally responsible and resilient.

What's Next?

UPYA will continue exploring opportunities for youth participation in climate action, environmental awareness, innovation and community-based initiatives.

The organisation also aims to connect young people with opportunities to learn, collaborate and develop ideas that can contribute to sustainable development.
