The Event

From ideas to practical opportunities.

From Ideas to Income brought young people together in Malela, Seme, for conversations and practical learning around entrepreneurship, financial literacy and turning ideas into sustainable opportunities.

The session created space for participants to learn, ask questions, share experiences and explore ways of creating income from their skills, ideas and resources.
