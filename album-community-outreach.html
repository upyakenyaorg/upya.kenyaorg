About the Album

Young people serving communities

UPYA believes that meaningful community transformation begins when people take responsibility for the places and communities they call home.

Through outreach activities, partnerships and volunteer initiatives, young people are encouraged to identify community needs and take practical steps towards addressing them.

Our Approach

Action that reaches people

Community outreach is more than an event. It is an opportunity to listen, understand local needs and work together towards practical solutions.

  • Community service and volunteer activities
  • Visits to vulnerable members and community institutions
  • Youth-led community initiatives
  • Civic and community awareness activities
  • Collaboration with local organisations and partners
  • Identifying community needs and supporting practical solutions

Communities grow when people participate
