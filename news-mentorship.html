Youth Mentorship Forum

23 September 2026 10:00 AM Seme

UPYA's Youth Mentorship Forum brings young people together with mentors and community voices to encourage learning, personal development, leadership and responsible participation in community development.

Full Story

The Youth Mentorship Forum provides a platform where young people can interact, ask questions, exchange experiences and learn from people with different experiences and perspectives.

Through open conversations and practical guidance, the forum seeks to help young people understand their potential and make informed decisions about education, careers, entrepreneurship, leadership and their role in society.

UPYA believes that mentorship should not be a one-time conversation. It should create lasting relationships through which young people can continue learning, receiving guidance and supporting one another.

What Happened

Mentorship Conversations Participants engaged in conversations focused on personal growth, education, careers and leadership.
Sharing Experiences Young people had an opportunity to share their experiences, questions and challenges.
Leadership Development The forum encouraged participants to think about responsible leadership and community participation.
Networking The event created opportunities for young people to connect with mentors and other participants.

Impact

Mentorship gives young people access to knowledge, encouragement and perspectives that can help them navigate important decisions. UPYA seeks to build a culture where young people learn from one another and from experienced members of society.

What's Next?

UPYA plans to continue developing mentorship opportunities that connect young people with experienced professionals, community leaders, educators and other positive role models.

The organisation will also continue encouraging young people to become mentors themselves by sharing knowledge and experiences with others.
