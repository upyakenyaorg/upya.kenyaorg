Chairperson's Message

Leadership is service, responsibility and action.

At Uwezo Pamoja Youths Association, we believe leadership is not simply about holding a position. It is about serving people, creating opportunities and helping young people discover and develop their potential.

Our commitment is to build an organization where young people are empowered to participate, equipped with practical skills, mentored by people who care, and nurtured to become responsible leaders in their communities.

We recognize that meaningful community transformation requires teamwork, integrity, transparency, accountability and equity. These values guide how we lead UPYA and how we serve the communities around us.

Hillary Onyango
Chairperson, UPYA
