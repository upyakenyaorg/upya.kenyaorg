Chairperson's Message

Leadership that serves with purpose.

"

At Uwezo Pamoja Youths Association, we believe that meaningful community transformation begins when young people are given the opportunity, knowledge, skills and confidence to participate.

Our leadership is committed to creating an environment where young people can learn, contribute, take responsibility and become part of solutions affecting their communities.

We will continue working together with our members, community leaders, partners and other stakeholders to build a generation that is empowered, equipped, mentored and nurtured to make a lasting difference.

Hillary Onyango Chairperson, UPYA

Governance & Accountability

Responsible leadership builds trust.

UPYA is committed to responsible leadership, proper stewardship of resources and transparent decision-making.

Our leadership works to ensure that activities remain aligned with the association's mission, values and the needs of the communities we serve.

Accountability is strengthened through clear responsibilities, proper communication, documentation and responsible management of organisational resources.
