About the Initiative

Young people taking action for the environment.

Climate Action is part of UPYA's commitment to environmental conservation, climate awareness and community resilience.

Through youth participation, environmental education and practical community activities, UPYA seeks to encourage young people to become responsible stewards of their communities and natural environment.

Focus

Environment & climate action

Participants

Youth, volunteers and community members

Approach

Awareness, action and environmental responsibility

Goal

Building resilient and sustainable communities

Our Story

Turning environmental awareness into action.

Climate change affects communities in many ways, making environmental awareness and local action increasingly important.

UPYA believes young people should not only learn about climate challenges but also participate in finding practical solutions within their communities.

Climate Action therefore creates opportunities for youth to learn, participate, volunteer and encourage others to take better care of the environment.
