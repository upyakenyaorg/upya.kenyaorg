The Story

Turning ideas into opportunities.

From Ideas to Income brought young people together to explore entrepreneurship, financial literacy and practical ways of developing ideas into opportunities.

The session encouraged participants to think creatively, understand the importance of financial planning and identify opportunities within their communities.

Through conversations, group activities and practical discussions, participants were encouraged to move from simply having ideas to taking meaningful steps towards implementing them.

What Happened

Key activities

Entrepreneurship

Participants explored entrepreneurship, identifying opportunities and developing practical business ideas.

Financial Literacy

Discussions focused on financial awareness, planning, saving and responsible use of money.

Group Discussions

Young people shared ideas, experiences and possible solutions to challenges facing them.

Impact

From conversation to action.

The engagement created space for young people to connect, learn and think practically about economic opportunities.
