UPYA Programmes

Creating opportunities. Building capacity. Empowering young people.

UPYA creates opportunities, builds capacity and empowers young people to develop their potential and transform their communities.

Our approach

Programmes designed around real community needs.

Uwezo Pamoja Youths Association implements programmes that respond to the opportunities, challenges and aspirations of young people and their communities. Through partnerships, mentorship, training, awareness and community action, we help young people gain the knowledge, skills, confidence and opportunities needed to create meaningful change.

Programme areas

Where we focus our work.

01

Youth Empowerment & Leadership

We equip young people with leadership skills, mentorship, civic knowledge and opportunities to participate meaningfully in community development and decision-making.

  • Leadership training
  • Youth mentorship
  • Civic engagement
  • Youth participation
02

Education & Mentorship

We support young people through school mentorship, career guidance, academic motivation and practical learning opportunities.

  • School mentorship
  • Career guidance
  • Academic motivation
  • Skills development
03

Economic Empowerment

We help young people develop the knowledge and skills needed to pursue entrepreneurship, employment and sustainable income-generating opportunities.

  • Entrepreneurship
  • Financial literacy
  • Employability skills
  • Income-generating initiatives
04

Health & Wellbeing

We promote awareness, healthy lifestyles and wellbeing among young people while creating spaces where health information can be shared openly and responsibly.

  • Health awareness
  • Healthy lifestyles
  • Youth wellbeing
  • Community awareness
05

GBV Awareness & Prevention

We raise awareness about gender-based violence, promote prevention and encourage communities to respond to GBV through education, awareness and appropriate support systems.

  • GBV awareness
  • Prevention education
  • Community dialogue
  • Referral to appropriate support
06

Environment & Climate Action

We engage young people in environmental conservation and climate action through education, tree planting and youth-led initiatives.

  • Tree planting
  • Climate education
  • Environmental conservation
  • Youth-led climate initiatives
07

Community Development

We work with communities to identify local needs and support initiatives that strengthen participation, cooperation and community-led development.

  • Community initiatives
  • Volunteerism
  • Community participation
  • Local development
08

Sports, Arts & Talent Development

We use sport, creativity and talent development to build confidence, discover potential, strengthen social cohesion and create positive spaces for young people.

  • Sports activities
  • Arts and creativity
  • Talent development
  • Social cohesion
