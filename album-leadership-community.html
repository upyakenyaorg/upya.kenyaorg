About the Initiative

Preparing young people to lead.

UPYA believes that young people have an important role to play in shaping the future of their communities.

Youth Leadership focuses on developing confidence, responsibility, communication, teamwork and civic participation among young people.

Focus

Youth leadership development

Participants

Young people and emerging leaders

Approach

Dialogue, mentorship and participation

Goal

Developing responsible community leaders

```
```
Our Story

Leadership begins with responsibility.

Leadership is not simply about holding a position. It is about serving others, taking responsibility and creating positive change.

UPYA provides spaces where young people can develop these qualities through mentorship, dialogue, community activities and practical leadership experiences.

By encouraging young people to participate in their communities today, UPYA seeks to nurture a generation prepared to take responsibility for tomorrow.
