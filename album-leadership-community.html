About the Album

Building the leaders of tomorrow

Leadership begins with responsibility, service and the willingness to create positive change.

UPYA creates opportunities for young people to develop leadership skills, understand their responsibilities and participate meaningfully in decisions and activities affecting their communities.

Leadership Highlights

At a glance

Participation

Creating spaces where young people can participate and express their ideas.

Dialogue

Encouraging open conversations about leadership, community and development.

Integrity

Promoting responsible leadership grounded in honesty, accountability and fairness.

Purpose

Helping young people understand their potential and use it to serve their communities.

Our Approach

Leadership through service

UPYA believes leadership is not simply about holding a position. It is about taking responsibility, listening to others and working towards positive change.

  • Youth leadership training and conversations
  • Civic education and community participation
  • Public speaking and communication development
  • Teamwork and collaborative decision-making
  • Mentorship and leadership guidance
  • Youth-led community initiatives

Our Vision

A generation ready to lead

We envision young people who are confident, responsible and equipped to contribute positively to their communities.

Through leadership development, mentorship and meaningful participation, UPYA seeks to nurture young people who lead with integrity, accountability and a commitment to service.
