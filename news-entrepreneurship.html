UPYA Hosts Youth Entrepreneurship Session

23 May 2026 | Malela Village

Young people participating in a UPYA community activity.

Introduction

On 23 May 2026, Uwezo Pamoja Youths Association brought young people together in Malela Village for a practical conversation on entrepreneurship, financial literacy and opportunities for youth.

The session was designed to create a space where young people could share ideas, learn from one another and explore practical approaches to building sustainable livelihoods.

Full Story

The event brought together young people interested in entrepreneurship and personal development. Discussions focused on the importance of identifying opportunities, developing useful skills and making informed financial decisions.

Participants were encouraged to look at the resources and abilities available within their communities while thinking creatively about solutions to everyday challenges.

What Happened

The programme included conversations around entrepreneurship, financial literacy, idea development and the practical steps young people can take when exploring income-generating opportunities.

Participants were also given an opportunity to exchange experiences, ask questions and discuss some of the challenges young people encounter when pursuing economic opportunities.

Impact

Beyond the individual lessons shared during the session, the activity helped create a stronger connection between young people and the wider community.

UPYA believes that when young people are equipped with knowledge, skills, confidence and meaningful opportunities, they can become active contributors to the development of their communities.

What's Next

UPYA will continue creating opportunities for young people to learn, connect and take action. Future activities will build on conversations around entrepreneurship, leadership, education and community development.
