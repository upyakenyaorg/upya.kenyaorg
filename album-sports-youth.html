About the Initiative

Sport as a pathway to youth connection.

Sports & Youth Engagement brings young people together through football, recreation and other community sporting activities.

UPYA uses sport as a platform for teamwork, discipline, friendship, leadership and meaningful youth participation in community life.

Focus

Sports and youth engagement

Participants

Youth, teams and community members

Approach

Teamwork, participation and inclusion

Goal

Empowering youth through positive engagement

Our Story

More than a game.

Sport has the power to bring people together across different backgrounds and create a shared sense of belonging.

UPYA creates spaces where young people can meet, compete, cooperate and build relationships through sporting activities.

Beyond the field, these interactions provide opportunities to promote leadership, teamwork, discipline, healthy relationships and community responsibility.
